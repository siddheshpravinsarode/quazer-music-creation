QUASAR SYNTH / QUICK START GUIDE
01
BOOT THE ENGINE
Click INITIALIZE SYSTEM in the top bar. Your browser needs permission to use audio. The status LED will turn green. This is required before any sound will play.
02
PICK YOUR KEY & SCALE
In the SCALE GATEKEEPER panel — choose a Root Key (e.g. C) and a Scale Mode (e.g. Minor Pentatonic). This locks all notes to only "allowed" frequencies. Click TRIGGER PITCH to hear a random note snap into the scale.
03
TEACH A MELODY (OPTIONAL BUT COOL)
In MELODY LEARNING MACHINE — type note names like C4 C4 G4 G4 A4 A4 G4 (that's Twinkle Twinkle). Click TRAIN TRANSITIONS to teach the AI your melody's note patterns. Click PLAY SONG to hear it. The Markov chain in Step 04 will now imitate your style.
04
LET THE AI COMPOSE FREELY
In PROBABILITY MATRIX — toggle Play Melody Stream ON to start an infinite AI-generated melody. The graph shows each note (state) and the probability arrows of which note comes next. Higher numbers in the matrix = more likely that transition. Load AMBIENT or CHAOTIC presets to change character.
05
GENERATE A FRACTAL SYMPHONY
In FRACTAL CELLULAR SYSTEM — set Iterations to GEN III or IV, then click EXPAND DNA to see the recursive structure. Hit PLAY SEQUENCE to hear the full fractal layout play as a timed composition. The timeline ticker scrolls with each step.
06
TRAIN THE RL SYNTH AGENT
In RL IMPROVISER — toggle Auto-Improvise and click 👍 REWARD or 👎 PENALIZE on transitions. Watch the Q-table heatmap update live as the synth learns which note-flows you prefer.
07
SHAPE YOUR SOUND
Use the oscilloscope bar at the top to change the Synth Wave (Triangle is warm, Sawtooth is harsh), adjust Cutoff to filter the brightness, and fine-tune Attack / Release timing. The waveform canvas will react live.
NOTE SYNTAX: Write notes as C4, G#3, Bb5 etc. Omit octave = defaults to octave 4. Separate with spaces.
